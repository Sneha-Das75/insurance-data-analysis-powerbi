# Insurance Data Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes insurance policy, customer, premium, coverage, and claim data to understand the overall performance of an insurance portfolio.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor key insurance metrics, analyze claim patterns, compare policy types, and identify differences across customer age groups and policy activity.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall claim, premium, and coverage amounts
- Understand claim status distribution
- Compare premium amounts across different policy types
- Analyze active and inactive policies
- Examine claim amounts across different age groups
- Compare claim outcomes across policy types
- Provide an interactive dashboard for exploring insurance performance

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Cleaning & Transformation

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following major KPIs:

- Total Claim Amount
- Total Premium Amount
- Total Coverage Amount
- Number of Claims
- Active vs Inactive Policies

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Premium Amount by Policy Type

The dashboard compares premium contributions across different insurance policy types including:

- Travel
- Health
- Auto
- Life
- Home

Travel insurance contributes the highest premium amount among the policy types shown in the dashboard.

### Claim Status Analysis

Claims are analyzed according to their current status:

- Rejected
- Settled
- Pending

This provides an overview of how claims are progressing and the proportion of claims that have been resolved or rejected.

### Active vs Inactive Policies

The dashboard provides a breakdown of active and inactive policies, helping assess the current state of the insurance portfolio.

### Claim Amount by Age Group

Claim amounts are compared across:

- Middle Aged
- Elder
- Young Adult

This helps identify differences in claim values across customer age segments.

### Claim Status by Policy Type

A detailed table compares pending, rejected, and settled claims across different policy types, allowing users to identify policy categories with higher claim activity.

## 🔍 Interactive Features

The dashboard includes filters for:

- Policy Number
- Customer ID
- Claim Number

These filters allow users to drill down into individual policies, customers, and claims.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Travel insurance contributes the highest premium amount among the displayed policy types.
- The majority of policies in the dashboard are active.
- Rejected claims represent the largest claim-status category among the displayed categories.
- Claim amounts vary considerably across customer age groups.
- Travel insurance shows substantial claim activity across pending, rejected, and settled claims.
